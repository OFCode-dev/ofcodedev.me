How counting works

RotPause is a small awareness tool, not a score about your brain or a promise to cure scrolling. You choose your personal limit and can dismiss prompts or stop counting.

Qualified videos and quick skips

A detected video qualifies after at least 750 milliseconds of stable visibility. A detected candidate replaced by a confirmed video transition before then is recorded as a quick skip. Quick skips appear separately and do not increase your qualified total, daily limit use or Wear total. Exiting, pausing or losing permission alone does not invent a skip.

There is no universal industry skip duration. The 0.75-second rule is this product’s choice. It is not the view-count definition used by any social platform. Ambiguous transitions may be missed, and several events from one swipe must be coalesced to avoid duplicate counts.

Before setup

You can browse RotPause without access. Counting requires the optional in-app consent and a separate Android Accessibility Service permission. In My plan, select supported apps. On Today, check whether counting is not set up, active, paused or missing permission.

Current compatibility

  • Adapters exist for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight.
  • TikTok and X fullscreen-video adapters are experimental and off by default. Normal X text timelines are outside the intended counter.
  • Current real social-app versions, Android vendors and interface languages have not yet completed the compatibility matrix. Internal synthetic tests are not proof of real-platform accuracy.
  • Third-party layout changes, comments, overlays, system power management or ambiguous pager events can affect detection.

What is stored

Daily per-app qualified totals, detected quick skips and duration are kept on the phone, with settings and consent metadata. Breathing history separately stores starts, completed sessions and recorded timer milliseconds from this feature onward; it is not a health score. Older than 90 calendar days is cleaned on the next run or recording. Export and history deletion are in Settings. Screen contents are not recorded. Optional watch sync is a separate choice and shares only a daily summary; see the privacy policy.

Report a mismatch

Use Settings → Get support to review a technical report, then copy it or open your email app. Include the social-app version, Android version, which screen was involved, expected count and observed count. Private messages, credentials or browsing history are not needed. No report is sent automatically.
