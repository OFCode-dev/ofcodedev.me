A little help with RotPause.

Contact contact@ofcodedev.me for support. Include your Android version, RotPause version and the supported app/version involved. Do not send passwords, messages or account credentials.

Include a diagnostic report

In Settings, tap Get support. Review the optional report, then choose Open email to include it in the message body, or Copy report. It contains device/app versions, permissions, adapter settings and recent technical events from this app process. It excludes viewing history, screenshots, messages and account details. You send the email yourself; nothing is sent automatically.

The counter is not increasing

Check that you have consented, enabled RotPause in Android Accessibility settings, left counting switched on and enabled the app in My plan. Open a supported short-video screen and remain on the video for at least 0.75 seconds. Detected quick skips appear separately and do not increase the qualified total or daily limit. Detection depends on the other app's version and interface; normal feeds and comments are not intended to count.

Wear OS

Install the matching RotPause watch app and enable optional sync in the phone Settings after reading its disclosure. The watch shows the latest received daily summary; cached and waiting labels matter. Pairing, a compatible watch and Google Play services are required. A failed or disconnected sync is not evidence that your count is zero. Turning sync off requests cache clearing on reconnection. For immediate removal on an offline watch, clear that watch app’s storage or uninstall it.

Counting rules, compatibility and known limits · Breathing safety and sources

Pause or disable RotPause

Use Pause counter, switch off live counters or reminders in Settings, or withdraw consent. Disable RotPause in Android Accessibility settings to turn the service off completely.

Breathing

The optional 4–2–8–2 timer lasts 1:22: two seconds of preparation and five complete cycles. You can stop at any time. Each start, including an early stop, uses one of three starts per local day. This product setting is not a medical dose or proof of suitability. Read the safety information before starting.

Delete your data

Settings → Delete all counting records removes the local history. RotPause has no online account or publisher usage database. Uninstalling removes that installation’s local app data. Clear the watch installation separately if needed. Settings and the latest consent choice remain after deleting history. Export includes daily history, settings and consent metadata. Exported copies are managed by you.
