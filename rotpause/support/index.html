A little help with RotPause.

Contact contact@ofcodedev.me for support. Include your Android version, RotPause version and the supported app/version involved. Do not send passwords, messages or account credentials.

The counter is not increasing

Check that you have consented, enabled RotPause in Android Accessibility settings, left counting switched on and enabled the app in My plan. Open a supported short-video screen. Detection depends on the other app's version and interface; normal feeds and comments are not intended to count.

Pause or disable RotPause

Use Pause counter, switch off live counters or reminders in Settings, or withdraw consent. Disable RotPause in Android Accessibility settings to turn the service off completely.

Delete your data

Settings → Delete all counting records removes the local history. RotPause has no online account or remote database. Uninstalling also removes local app data. Exported copies are managed by you.
