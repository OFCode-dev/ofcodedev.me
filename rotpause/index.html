Can I download RotPause now?

Not yet. This is a release preview, with no live Google Play listing or purchase link. The planned model is a US$1 one-time paid download, with final local prices shown by the store.

Why 0.75 seconds?

It is RotPause’s product rule for stable detected visibility, not a universal skip duration or a platform’s official view definition. Detected faster transitions appear separately as quick skips. Ambiguous transitions can be missed.

What does accessibility access see?

With consent, RotPause temporarily processes supported interface identifiers, selected navigation labels and scroll events. It does not record messages, passwords, video, audio or screenshots. It is not a disability support tool. Read the full explanation.

How is breathing practice recorded?

Starts, completed sessions and recorded timer time are saved locally from this update. Earlier sessions are not reconstructed. Time includes preparation; an interrupted app process can leave its last interval unrecorded. These are timer-use statistics, not a health or breathing-quality score.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings includes export and local-history deletion. History older than 90 calendar days is cleaned on the next run or recording. Settings, consent and the current breathing allowance remain after deleting history. Watch sync has an off-and-clear control; a disconnected watch can keep its latest summary until it reconnects or you clear that installation.

Is the breathing timer right for everyone?

No suitability or medical benefit is promised. Follow the safety notice and stop when uncomfortable. If pregnant or affected by a significant heart, lung or other medical condition, ask a qualified clinician before breath-holding exercises. Severe breathing difficulty or sudden/persistent chest pain requires local emergency medical help. Safety guidance and sources.
