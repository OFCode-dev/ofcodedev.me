PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 28 OCTOBER 2026 · APP 1.0.5

Your history stays
local by default.

RotPause is an Android short-video counter developed by OFCode. This policy explains the information processed by the app. Contact us at contact@ofcodedev.me.

Information processed on your device

When you explicitly consent and enable its Android Accessibility Service, RotPause detects supported short-video screens and scrolling in the Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, TikTok and X apps you choose. It transiently processes interface resource identifiers and selected navigation-control labels to identify those screens. It does not record messages, passwords, video content, audio or screenshots.

From version 1.0.1, RotPause stores the date, app category, qualified short-video count, separate detected quick-skip count and precise duration locally in its app storage. It also stores settings and your latest accessibility-consent choice, notice version and UTC grant or withdrawal timestamps. The app also keeps its latest local-day breathing allowance (day, number of starts and reserved duration) so that daily limits survive a restart. A start reserves a 1:22 session, including an early stop. This allowance is replaced when a session starts on a different local day, or removed when app data is cleared/uninstalled; deleting video history keeps the allowance. It is included in the full local-data export, but not in support diagnostics. The unused hourly totals from the earlier preview are removed on upgrade to 1.0.1; new hourly history is not recorded. TikTok and fullscreen X detection are experimental and off by default. Current real-app compatibility validation is incomplete.

From version 1.0.5, breathing history also stores each local day’s starts, completed sessions and recorded timer milliseconds. It starts with this feature; earlier completions are not reconstructed. Timer time includes preparation and does not measure breathing quality or health. Five-second checkpoints limit disk writes; process interruption can leave the last interval unrecorded. This history follows the same 90-calendar-day cleanup and is included in full export and local-history deletion. It is excluded from support reports and watch sync.

Optional paired-watch transfer

Wear OS synchronization is off by default and has a separate explanation and choice in Settings. If enabled, RotPause sends the date, today’s qualified total, personal daily limit and update time, with protocol version and enabled flag, to your paired watch using Google Play services. Per-app history, quick skips, breathing allowance, accessibility identifiers and screen contents are not included. Google’s Data Layer can use Bluetooth or Google servers as a relay; Google documents end-to-end encryption for cloud relay. See the official Data Layer description.

The watch stores only its latest received summary in private app storage. Older dates or updates more than 15 minutes old are labelled cached/stale; no data is shown as a waiting state, not a real zero. Turning sync off queues a clearing update. A disconnected watch may retain its summary until it reconnects and receives that update. Uninstalling only the phone app does not erase a watch installation; clear or uninstall each separately when needed.

No advertising or publisher telemetry

RotPause has no accounts, advertising, subscriptions, analytics SDKs or publisher usage backend. Both app manifests request no internet permission; optional watch transfer nevertheless uses Google Play services and may leave the phone through its transport. OFCode does not receive this daily summary through an app backend. We do not sell your data. If you explicitly choose Export, Android lets you select the destination application. The export contains daily history, settings and consent metadata; that destination's own privacy practices apply to what you share.

Accessibility permission and your control

The Accessibility Service is used only for the disclosed counter functionality, optional live-counter overlays and optional dismissible break prompts. RotPause is not a disability support tool. It performs a navigation action only if you tap the Leave short videos button.

You can explore the app without granting accessibility permission. Pause counting, turn overlays or reminders off, select which supported apps to track, or withdraw consent in RotPause Settings. You can also disable the service in Android Accessibility settings or uninstall RotPause at any time.

Retention, export and deletion

History older than 90 calendar days is removed when RotPause next runs or records activity. No background timer cleans dormant app storage. You can remove counting, quick-skip and breathing history in RotPause Settings; settings and the latest consent choice remain. Withdrawing consent stops counting without deleting past records. Settings and consent metadata remain until Android app data is cleared or RotPause is uninstalled. System cloud backup and device transfer of RotPause's app data are disabled in both app configurations. The latest safety-information acknowledgment and selected language are also kept as settings until app data is cleared/uninstalled. Optional watch cache and transport are described above. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Any copy you choose to export is under your control and is not deleted by RotPause.

Storage safeguards

Android app storage protections apply. RotPause does not add database encryption. SQLite secure deletion is enabled in 1.0.1, but neither logical deletion nor uninstall is a guarantee of forensic erasure from flash storage or copies outside the app. Keep your device locked and up to date.

Support messages and this website

Get support can prepare an optional local diagnostic report containing the app/Android version, device manufacturer/model, permission and counter settings, selected adapters and up to 30 recent technical events from the current app process. These events remain in memory and are cleared when that process ends; they contain event/error class names, not exception messages, stack traces or screen content. The report excludes viewing history/counts, screenshots, messages, device identifiers, IP addresses and account details. You review the report before copying it or opening an email draft. RotPause does not send it automatically. If you choose to email us, we receive the address and information you put in your message, including any diagnostic report you send. Send only what is needed to explain the issue; do not send passwords, private messages or account credentials. The RotPause website pages contain no forms, advertising, analytics scripts, remote fonts or application-set cookies. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages; its provider may process request metadata such as IP address and browser information to serve and secure pages, under its own privacy statement. Support email and hosting are separate from the app’s default local counting. Support-message handling, provider arrangements and final publisher legal disclosures are still being finalized for this unreleased preview.

Privacy requests

Contact contact@ofcodedev.me to ask about information held by the publisher, request access, correction or deletion, or raise an objection or other right available under applicable law. Do not send private app history to prove your identity. We do not remotely hold or retrieve your local app database; use the local export, deletion and permission controls for that data. Support correspondence and purchase records, if any, are separate. Depending on applicable law, you may also complain to your competent data protection authority.

Preview status

RotPause has not been publicly released through Google Play. The publisher legal identity, launch regions and activity-specific legal grounds and vendor arrangements must be finalized before a commercial launch. This page is a factual explanation of the preview, not a GDPR or KVKK compliance certification.

Children and wellbeing

RotPause is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect children's information. Counts are estimates dependent on third-party app versions and layouts, rather than official video-view data. RotPause does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Policy changes

We will update this page and the in-app policy if the app's data practices change. Any new use requiring consent will be presented before it is enabled.
